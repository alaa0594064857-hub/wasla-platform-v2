## Mandatory owner review before listing

- Confirm legal supplier identity and platform protection.
- Obtain a current written quote for the exact 1L variant, including currency and quote expiry.
- Confirm stock, MOQ, sample availability and sample cost.
- Obtain and verify food-contact test reports/certificates relevant to each destination market; do not rely on generic CE/EN71 claims.
- Request real photos/video and test a sample for leaks, capacity, material, odour and insulation performance.
- Confirm whether supplier can ship one unit directly to each customer destination, supported countries, shipping cost, delivery estimate, tracking, customs handling and returns.
- Confirm rights to use product photos and marketing claims.
- Compare at least two exact-specification offers and calculate landed cost before owner-approved sale pricing.
