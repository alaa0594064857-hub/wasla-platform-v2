# WASLA Store demand priorities — 12 November 2026

## Purpose and confidence

This is a dated market-priority brief for the owner and WASLA AI team. It is not live marketplace ranking data, a supplier verification, a profit forecast, or proof that a particular SKU will sell. The sources below report category revenue, survey purchase incidence, marketplace searches, or best-seller snapshots; those measures are not interchangeable. Use them to decide what to investigate first, then validate exact products, variants, supplier evidence, delivered cost, returns and destination eligibility before listing anything.

## Investigation order for WASLA

These are research priorities, not published products:

1. **Mobile accessories and audio** — chargers, screen protectors and Bluetooth audio. Strong cross-market electronics relevance and Amazon category examples. Verify electrical safety, compatibility, brand authenticity, warranty and exact model.
2. **Men's sports shoes** — clear Turkey search signal and broad US footwear demand. High sizing/returns risk; investigate only suppliers with reliable size charts, authentic goods and a workable returns route.
3. **Smartwatches and wearable accessories** — explicit Turkey search signal and cross-market electronics demand. Verify supported phones/OS, battery claims, app availability, radio certifications, warranty and language.
4. **Small home appliances** — espresso machines, cordless vacuums and robot vacuums show Turkey purchase signals; home/furniture categories matter in US ecommerce. Higher shipping, warranty, damage and returns risk.
5. **Photo/video creator accessories** — fastest-growing photo/camera category in the cited Hepsiburada first-half report; consider microphones, tripods and lighting only after compatibility and safety checks.
6. **Beauty and personal care** — persistent US online category demand and Amazon best-seller examples. Defer ingestibles, medical claims and products requiring special regulatory handling; require ingredient, labeling, importer and authenticity evidence.
7. **Auto accessories** — Turkey platform growth signal, but compatibility, installation and vehicle-specific fitment create material return and liability risks.

## Required next steps for the AI Store Manager

1. Re-rank these categories separately for US and Türkiye, stating which evidence is search interest, purchase incidence, revenue, or growth.
2. Identify exact product archetypes and variants to investigate; do not invent SKU demand, prices, reviews, stock or supplier details.
3. Find only sources listed in WASLA's approved data-source registry or owner-provided evidence. No scraping, access-control bypass, or reuse of Amazon images, descriptions, ratings or prices without authorized API/permission.
4. For every proposed source offer, collect supplier identity evidence, original product URL, exact specifications, MOQ, single-unit direct-shipping confirmation, destination coverage, shipping cost/time, quote expiry, warranty and returns terms.
5. Calculate landed cost and destination-specific margin only from documented values. Include taxes, duties, payment fees, returns and advertising as explicit inputs; leave unknowns unknown.
6. Keep all research private. No product publication, supplier approval, customer price, order, payment or purchase without the existing owner-controlled approval gates.

## Release rule

A high-demand category is not enough to open sales. The official store remains in draft until there are real products with verified source offers, valid destination-specific approved prices, confirmed fulfilment, and a legally eligible payment/checkout path.
