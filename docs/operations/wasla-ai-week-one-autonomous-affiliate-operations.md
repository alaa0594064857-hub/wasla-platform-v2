# WASLA AI — Week 1 Autonomous Affiliate Operations

Effective date: 2026-11-12
Owner instruction: operate continuously without requiring a daily owner prompt; send a daily activity report and accept strategic direction at the weekly review.

## Week 1 scope
- Affiliate commerce only.
- Start with the US Amazon Associates program already recorded for WASLA, while treating the application as pending until Amazon confirms final approval.
- Research additional legitimate affiliate programs relevant to the United States and Türkiye. Keep each program, country, currency, eligibility, attribution window, commission terms, application state, and source evidence separate.
- Do not assume Amazon US authorization applies to Amazon Türkiye or another marketplace.
- Do not add unrelated inventory, supplier purchasing, dropshipping, or non-affiliate commerce during this first week.

## General Manager workflow
1. Maintain a prioritized research queue across market research, product analysis, affiliate-program research, catalogue preparation, sales, localization, finance, logistics/fulfilment implications, and quality/compliance.
2. Create traceable tasks with source URLs, access date, marketplace, evidence confidence, and unresolved questions.
3. Research product categories and candidate products using permitted, attributable sources. Distinguish observed demand signals from estimates and editorial judgment.
4. Prepare bilingual catalogue records only when product identity and source facts are traceable. Do not invent prices, discounts, ratings, stock, shipping, product images, commission rates, or product links.
5. Add an affiliate product to the official-store catalogue only when the program and marketplace permit it, a valid affiliate destination URL and required disclosure are available, product data/image rights are established, and required fields pass validation. Otherwise retain it as a private draft with an explicit blocker.
6. No purchases, paid subscriptions, supplier commitments, customer messaging, financial transactions, or changes that incur charges.
7. Record every task transition and result for owner audit.

## Reporting
- Produce one daily owner report: completed research, candidates evaluated, products prepared, products actually added, source links, unresolved blockers, and next priorities. Never count drafts as published/added products.
- Prepare a weekly review agenda and summary for the owner. The owner provides strategic direction at the weekly review; routine research continues between reviews.
- Reports must be based on recorded task events and actual catalogue state, not invented progress.

## Safety and release gates
- The existing AI team task system is currently advisory and manually initiated. This document does not itself create a scheduler, external browsing capability, affiliate API connection, or publishing permission.
- Public product publication must remain blocked until the catalogue's affiliate-specific fields, link validation, disclosure, source provenance, and image-rights checks are implemented and tested.
- Amazon Associates application status remains pending final review until the program confirms otherwise.
- If a source cannot be accessed or a program's terms cannot be verified, mark the item blocked and continue with other eligible research.
- The owner retains control over financial commitments and any action that could charge money.
