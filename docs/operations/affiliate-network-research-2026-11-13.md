# WASLA Affiliate Network Research — 2026-11-13

## Decision summary

Evaluate **Admitad first** and **Awin second** as publisher-network candidates for WASLA. This is a research shortlist only: no account has been created, no publisher application has been submitted, and no advertiser program has been approved. Do not add either network to the live affiliate-program registry or enable outbound tracking until WASLA's publisher identity, legal/tax residence, promotional-space details, payout information, network approval, advertiser-level approval, and applicable terms have been verified.

## Admitad

- Official publisher signup instructions: https://support.mitgo.com/hc/en-us/articles/33314787170578-How-to-sign-up-for-Admitad-as-a-publisher
- Publisher signup is available by registration form or supported social login.
- During registration, the country of residence is detected automatically and cannot be changed after registration. The applicant should confirm the detected country is correct before submitting.
- The applicant must accept the publisher Terms of Use and complete the onboarding survey and ad-space setup.
- A publisher account does not itself authorize every advertiser campaign. The publisher must review and join individual affiliate programs.
- Official publisher overview describes a global advertiser network and XML product feeds, but actual advertiser availability, feed permissions, commission rates, payout rules, and country restrictions must be confirmed inside the approved publisher account: https://www.admitad.com/affiliates/
- General terms: https://www.admitad.com/terms-of-use/

## Awin

- Official publisher requirements: https://success.awin.com/s/article/What-are-the-requirements-for-joining-the-Awin-network
- Applicants must be at least 18 and have a promotional space, such as a website, blog, social channel, newsletter, or another accepted platform.
- The application asks for accurate details about the promotional space and is reviewed by Awin.
- Tax information and bank details are needed for payouts, even if they are not required at initial signup.
- Network approval is separate from acceptance into individual advertiser programs. The publisher must apply to each relevant advertiser program and follow its own terms.
- Official application walkthrough: https://success.awin.com/s/article/How-do-I-join-Awin-as-a-Publisher
- Publisher terms depend on tax residence and the programs used: https://www.awin.com/us/publisher-terms

## WASLA onboarding requirements before applying

1. Confirm whether WASLA applies as an individual publisher or a registered legal entity; do not guess.
2. Confirm the applicant's actual country of legal/tax residence. Do not infer it from a temporary IP or device location.
3. Confirm the applicant is at least 18 and authorized to represent WASLA if applying for the business.
4. Use https://wasla.trade as the promotional space only after its public pages, privacy notice, contact information, and affiliate disclosure are live and accurate.
5. Prepare an honest description of WASLA's shopping discovery and editorial content. Do not claim a large audience, traffic, sales, or existing partnerships without evidence.
6. Review network and advertiser-level restrictions for product feeds, price/image usage, paid search, email, social traffic, trademark bidding, and geographic eligibility.
7. Add a provider to the production registry only after the application is actually submitted and its real status is known. Mark the program active only after written approval and terms review.
8. Store provider credentials server-side only. Never paste passwords, tax identifiers, bank details, or API secrets into source code or chat.

## Current WASLA technical gate

The affiliate registry currently contains the Amazon US Associates record only, with status pending approval. The public affiliate catalog has no published commission items. The existing editorial discovery cards are separate from affiliate offers and must not be represented as commission-bearing products. Keep public affiliate publishing and outbound affiliate links disabled until an approved program, authorized links/content source, localized disclosure, and hosted tests are all verified.
